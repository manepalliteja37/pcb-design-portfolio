Please note the following important considerations when using the resistive soil moisture sensor:
~Soil moisture sensor values may fluctuate depending on moisture levels, with significant variations between dry and saturated conditions.
~Soil Moisture Sensor values may fluctuate in dry conditions due to residual water particles or moisture retained on the sensor after immersion.
~Prolonged exposure to water or consistent moisture can affect sensor readings.
